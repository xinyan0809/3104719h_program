How it works

Your body is the controller

PosePlay turns movements detected by a standard webcam into simple browser game controls.

  1. Choose a game

    Pick Fruit Catch, Target Shot or Body Dodge from the rotating cards.

  2. Start your camera

    Press Start Game and allow camera access so MediaPipe can detect your pose.

  3. Move and play

    Follow the short instructions and use your wrists, right hand or whole body.

🔒 Webcam frames and body coordinates are processed locally in your browser.
